A matter is now a hub marked `matter: true` in its frontmatter, not every hub that is not a responsibility. A matter is something going on in your owner's life that will end, has a state that moves, and is named the way they would name it if asked what is going on in their life. Topics, context notes, portrait pages, guides and the pointer an adopted responsibility left behind are hubs, not matters. Until you mark them, no hub is a matter, so the desk is empty until this is done.

1. List your hubs (`kind: hub`, outside `Responsibilities/`). For each, decide: is it itself something going on that will end, at the grain your owner would name? A trip, a move, a playthrough, a job search, a purchase, a project they would name.
2. Mark each one that is by adding `matter: true` to its frontmatter. Leave everything else as it is: a topic stays a topic, and a step inside something larger is not marked.
3. Where several narrow hubs are steps of one larger matter (a character build inside a first playthrough of the game, one service inside a project), mark the larger one, creating it as a hub at the vault root with `kind: hub` and `matter: true` when none exists, then mark each narrow one and run `brain matters --merge "<narrow>" --into "<larger>"` so its work and history move.
4. Do not mark a hub whose `## Now` describes your own machinery, another intellect's reports or a responsibility's work; those lines stay but no longer reach the desk.
5. `brain validate`, then `brain commit -m "migrate(<date>): mark matters" --push`, then `brain migrate-done`.
