The night now ends with a doctor: a routine after night-after whose pre-run script, `brain doctor check`, fixes what has one right fix and wakes the doctor only for what needs judgment, and reports what only a person can fix to the operators in `brain.toml`'s `operator_user_ids`.

1. Run `brain install-skills` and `brain install-scripts`, then `brain schedule init` to add the `doctor` routine.
2. Run `brain doctor check` once and read what it prints, without acting on it: the doctor runs tonight.
3. `brain commit -m "migrate(<date>): schedule the doctor" --push`, then `brain migrate-done`.
